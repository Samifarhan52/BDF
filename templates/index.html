How LifePulse Operates

Pure HTML, CSS, and SQLite engineering connecting clinical centers, donors, and urgent patients in under 60 seconds.

01

1. Register & Stock Items

Hospitals register facilities and stock granular blood item packets (PRBC, Whole Blood, Platelets) directly into the SQLite database.

02

2. Dynamic Dropdown Requests

Medical staff post urgent needs by selecting registered facilities via dynamic dropdowns with automated phone and city resolution.

03

3. Instant Stock Fulfillment

The matching engine compares needs against real-time blood bank inventory and allows 1-click dispensing and donor dispatch.
