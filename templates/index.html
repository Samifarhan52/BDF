How LifePulse Works

Connecting donors and critical patients with real-time medical logic in under 60 seconds.

Search or Post Need

02

Compatibility Engine

Our medical algorithm maps ABO/Rh compatibility rules in real time (e.g. O- universal donors), ensuring zero wasted time on incompatible types.

03

Direct Life Connection

Direct phone calls, WhatsApp pings, and hospital navigation links allow instant coordination when every single second counts.
