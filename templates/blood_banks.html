SQLite Relational Architecture for Academic Demonstration

Each blood bank entity (blood_banks table) maintains a 1-to-many relationship with blood_inventory rows. Adding new items or fulfilling emergency requests directly updates SQLite records via atomic SQL transactions.

Table: blood_banks Stores facility ID, legal name, affiliated hospital, city, direct emergency telephone, and operational coverage hours.
Table: blood_inventory Stores item units partitioned by blood_bank_id, blood_group, and component_type (PRBC, Platelets, FFP).
Fulfillment Flow When an emergency request is matched, units are deducted from blood_inventory and updated in urgent_requests.
